warpgate panes

Usage:
  warpgate panes <command> [flags]

Commands:
  split     Split the focused pane
  close     Close the focused pane
  maximize  Maximize or restore the focused pane
  focus     Move focus to a neighboring pane
  grow      Move the divider of the focused pane
  swap      Swap the focused pane with a neighbor
  to-tab    Move the focused pane to a new tab

Run warpgate panes <command> --help for details.
