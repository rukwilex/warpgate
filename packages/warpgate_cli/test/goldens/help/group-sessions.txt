warpgate sessions

Usage:
  warpgate sessions <command> [flags]

Commands:
  list   List terminal sessions open in the app
  open   Open a terminal on a host in the app
  close  Close a terminal session and its pane
  focus  Bring a session to the front in the app
  send   Type text into a session. Use \r for Enter
  read   Print the last lines of a session

Run warpgate sessions <command> --help for details.
