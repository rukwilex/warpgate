warpgate transfers

Usage:
  warpgate transfers <command> [flags]

Commands:
  list    List transfers from the app and the CLI
  watch   Follow transfers until they finish
  pause   Pause a transfer
  resume  Resume a paused transfer
  cancel  Cancel a queued or running transfer
  retry   Run a failed or cancelled transfer again
  clear   Remove finished transfers from the list

Run warpgate transfers <command> --help for details.
